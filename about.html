About Dar-e-Arqam Online Quran Academy

Connecting hearts with the Holy Quran across borders through dedicated online tutoring.

Dar-e-Arqam Online Quran Academy was established with a sincere vision: to connect Muslim families and aspiring Quran learners worldwide with trustworthy, compassionate, and structured Quranic education directly from the comfort of their homes.

Named after the historic Dar-e-Arqam — the blessed house where the early companions of the Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) gathered to learn the revelations of the Holy Quran — our academy strives to embody that same spirit of sincerity, patience, and devotion to sacred knowledge.

We welcome students from Pakistan and across the globe, including the UK, USA, Canada, Europe, Australia, and the Gulf. Whether your child is taking their very first steps with Noorani Qaida, or you are an adult seeking to refine your Tajweed or memorize the Holy Book, our academy is honored to walk beside you on this noble journey.

Sincerity (Ikhlas)

Teaching the Book of Allah purely for the pleasure of Allah and the benefit of our students.

Patience & Care

Encouraging every learner with gentle guidance, no matter their beginning proficiency.

Global Accessibility

Bridging geographical boundaries so distance never hinders authentic Quranic learning.
