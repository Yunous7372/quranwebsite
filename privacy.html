Privacy Policy

Dar-e-Arqam Online Quran Academy

1. Sincere Commitment to Privacy

At Dar-e-Arqam Online Quran Academy, we consider the privacy of our students and their families to be an Amanah (a sacred trust). This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it in accordance with moral, Islamic, and legal data protection principles.

2. Information We Collect

When you register for a Free Trial Class, contact us via WhatsApp, or send an inquiry, we collect only the necessary details required to coordinate your learning:

  • Parent or Student Name
  • WhatsApp Contact Number and phone information
  • Student's age and grade level (to assign the right curriculum)
  • Preferred course, study timings, and timezone / country

3. Respect for Photographs & Video Recordings

In strict adherence to Islamic decorum and personal modesty, we do not publish or publicly share personal photographs or video recordings of students or teachers. Live 1-on-1 sessions are strictly private between the instructor and student, with parents welcomed and encouraged to monitor their children's classes at any time.

4. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to schedule trial classes, coordinate lesson timings, send student progress reports to parents, and provide educational support. We never sell, rent, trade, or distribute your personal contact details to third-party advertisers or commercial entities.

5. Contacting the Academy Administration

If you have any questions or wish to update or delete your contact records from our system, please reach out directly via WhatsApp or email at info@darearqamquran.com.
