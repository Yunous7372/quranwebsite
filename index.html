Our Courses

Our Sacred Courses

Comprehensive, structured Quranic and Islamic education tailored for every age and learning pace.

Beginner

Noorani Qaida

The foundational primer for Arabic alphabet mastery, letter recognition, correct pronunciation, and foundational phonetics designed for beginners and young children.

  • Arabic letter articulation (Makharij)
  • Connecting letters & joint shapes
  • Harakaat, Sukoon, and Madd rules
Beginner

Madani Qaida

A structured, beginner-friendly curriculum focusing on correct articulation, vowel signs, and essential phonetics to prepare students for seamless Quran recitation.

  • Comprehensive phonetics drills
  • Tanween, Jazm, and Tashdeed exercises
  • Clear pronunciation guidelines
Intermediate

Quran Reading (Nazra)

Fluent and continuous recitation of the Holy Quran with correct pauses, smooth phrasing, and guided practice under experienced Quran teachers.

  • Fluent reading Surah Al-Fatiha to An-Nas
  • Waqf (stopping) and resumption rules
  • Daily recitation practice & fluency
Advanced

Quran with Tajweed

Master the authentic rules of Tajweed including Makharij, Ghunnah, Ikhfa, Idgham, and Qalqalah to recite the Quran with elegance and divine reverence.

  • Deep study of Makharij (articulation)
  • Noon Sakinah & Meem Sakinah rules
  • Melodious, respectful recitation
Dedicated

Quran Memorization (Hifz-ul-Quran)

A disciplined, step-by-step Hifz program with customized daily memorization targets, rigorous revision routines, and motivational mentorship.

  • Sabaq (new lesson memorization)
  • Sabqi (recent lessons revision)
  • Manzil (long-term cumulative revision)
All Ages

Basic Islamic Studies

Nurturing young minds with essential Islamic knowledge, the Five Pillars of Islam, fundamental beliefs, beautiful Seerah of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH), and Islamic manners (Adab).

  • Six Kalimahs & Articles of Faith
  • Five Pillars of Islam explained simply
  • Islamic manners, ethics, and character
Essential

Daily Duas & Salah Learning

Hands-on instruction on performing Wudu and Salah correctly with translation, plus essential daily Masnoon Duas for morning, evening, and everyday occasions.

  • Step-by-step practical Salah guide
  • Complete Wudu demonstration & rulings
  • Daily Masnoon Duas with meanings

Our Sacred Mission

Making Authentic Quranic Education Accessible Worldwide

Dar-e-Arqam Online Quran Academy was established with a sincere vision: to connect Muslim families and aspiring Quran learners worldwide with trustworthy, compassionate, and structured Quranic education directly from the comfort of their homes.

Named after the historic Dar-e-Arqam — the blessed house where the early companions of the Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) gathered to learn the revelations of the Holy Quran — our academy strives to embody that same spirit of sincerity, patience, and devotion to sacred knowledge.

We welcome students from Pakistan and across the globe, including the UK, USA, Canada, Europe, Australia, and the Gulf. Whether your child is taking their very first steps with Noorani Qaida, or you are an adult seeking to refine your Tajweed or memorize the Holy Book, our academy is honored to walk beside you on this noble journey.

Sincerity (Ikhlas)

Teaching the Book of Allah purely for the pleasure of Allah and the benefit of our students.

Patience & Care

Encouraging every learner with gentle guidance, no matter their beginning proficiency.

Global Accessibility

Bridging geographical boundaries so distance never hinders authentic Quranic learning.
