Dar-e-Arqam Online Quran Academy Guidelines

1. General Overview

Welcome to Dar-e-Arqam Online Quran Academy. By registering for our free trial classes or regular courses, parents and students agree to observe the guidelines, respectful conduct, and schedules outlined herein.

2. Free Trial Class Policy

We offer a 100% free trial class for every new student. The free trial session carries zero obligation to enroll and requires no credit card details. Its sole purpose is to allow the student and parents to experience the teacher's methodology and evaluate the class setup.

3. Class Timings & Punctuality

Students are expected to join their designated online classroom punctually at the mutually agreed schedule. If a student is unable to attend a class due to illness or personal necessity, the parent or student should inform the teacher or academy coordinator via WhatsApp at least 2 to 4 hours in advance to arrange a makeup session where feasible.

4. Respectful Learning Environment

We are committed to maintaining a dignified, courteous, and polite Islamic learning atmosphere. Our teachers treat every child and adult student with warmth, patience, and encouraging words. In turn, students are expected to show reverence for the Holy Quran, its recitation rules, and their teachers.

5. Parent Supervision & Feedback

Parents of young children are welcome to sit in or quietly observe lessons at any time. Our teachers provide periodic progress updates so parents can follow their child's Quranic growth with confidence and joy.
